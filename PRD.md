# Product Requirements Document: SoloScribe

## 1. Executive Summary
**SoloScribe** is an AI-native strategic co-pilot designed specifically for solo founders and lean teams. It moves beyond simple text generation to provide high-fidelity strategic leverage, transforming chaotic founder "brain-dumps" into board-ready strategies, marketing loops, and revenue projections.

## 2. Target Audience
- **Solo Founders:** Individuals managing all aspects of a startup and needing deep strategic support.
- **Micro-SaaS Builders:** Developers looking to professionalize their marketing and business logic.
- **Content Entrepreneurs:** Creators building complex ecosystems around their personal brand.

## 3. Problem Statement
Solo founders often suffer from "Execution Debt"—they are so focused on daily tasks that they lack the cognitive space for deep strategic planning. Most AI tools provide generic templates; they don't provide the *integrated intelligence* needed to connect strategy to revenue, marketing, and compliance.

## 4. Solution Overview
SoloScribe provides a unified "Strategic Core" powered by the **Inklo Engine**. It features a friendly, helpful mascot (Inklo) and a neo-brutalist interface that emphasizes data sovereignty, rapid iteration, and high-bandwidth thought capture.

---

## 5. Core Features

### 5.1. Strategy Engine (The Brain)
- **Input:** High-bandwidth text/voice feed for founder thoughts.
- **Output:** Structured strategic frameworks (SWOT, MoSCoW, Value Propositions).
- **Inklo Integration:** Real-time feedback from the Inklo mascot during drafting.

### 5.2. Keynote Module (The Polish)
- Professional refinement of raw strategy into presentation narratives.
- Export-ready copy for pitch decks and landing pages.

### 5.3. Marketing Loop Designer
- AI-generated channel strategies (Viral Loops, ICP targeting).
- Audience sentiment analysis and keyword generation.

### 5.4. Compliance Shield
- Automated regulatory audits (GDPR, CCPA alignment).
- Ethical AI boundary checking for market plans.

### 5.5. Revenue Engine (Projections)
- Dynamic monetization simulation models.
- Growth trajectory charting using standard SaaS metrics.

### 5.6. AI Audit Engine
- Vulnerability scanning for business logic.
- Competitive gap identification and pivot advice.

---

## 6. User Experience & Design
- **Aesthetic:** Neo-Brutalist / Y2K (High contrast, bold borders, vibrant accents).
- **Mascot:** Inklo — A bouncy, metallic yellow 3D sphere with nerdy glasses.
- **Onboarding:** Guided tutorial for new founders.
- **Support:** Persistent Inklo Chatbot for support, bug reporting, and feedback.

---

## 7. Technical Requirements
- **Frontend:** React 18+, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Motion (Framer).
- **Backend/Auth:** Firebase (Authentication, Firestore).
- **AI Intelligence:** Gemini-powered Inklo Engine (Grounding & Reasoning).
- **State Management:** Zustand (Persisted state for founder preferences).

## 8. Success Metrics
- **Strategic Velocity:** Time taken from thought-dump to board-ready plan.
- **Retention:** Weekly active founders (WAU) returning for "Strategy Audits."
- **NPS:** Founder satisfaction with the "Inklo" mascot's helpfulness.

## 9. Roadmap
- **Phase 1 (Current):** Core Engine + Auth + Basic Tabs.
- **Phase 2:** Inklo_Net Expansion (Real-time market feed integration).
- **Phase 3:** High-fidelity Revenue Simulations & Advanced Export Tools.
- **Phase 4:** Inklo Community Loops (Founder-to-founder strategic benchmarking).
